# Two-minute review demo

Record your own screen and narration while you actually review. The assessment asks to show a few proposals with evidence, approve one, reject one, and show an approved change in its CRM browser. Do not approve or reject an item merely to stage a demo: explain your actual decision.

## Suggested flow

**0:00–0:20 — Scope and source completeness**

“I built a local reconciliation workflow that reads the operator website and the full CRM. The website directory contains 34 communities, but the homepage links to Findlay, so the crawler discovers 35. The initial scan read 121 CRM accounts.”

**0:20–0:50 — A clear correction**

Search for **Portsmouth** or **Ashtabula**. Show the website address beside the CRM record, expand match signals, and explain the exact difference. If you agree with the evidence, preview the selected operations, then click **Approve and execute these operations**. Read the record back and open the assessment's CRM browser to show the changed ZIP or street.

**0:50–1:20 — An uncertain case and rejection**

Open an identity-review proposal, such as **Kettering**. Show the same-address candidates and explain the missing corroboration. If the evidence is insufficient to choose an account or duplicate outcome, reject the proposal and record why. If you agree with that proposal, approve it instead and choose a different proposal you genuinely disagree with for the required rejection example. A rejection makes no CRM API writes and remains decided on future identical scans.

**1:20–1:45 — Billing preservation**

Open **Tiffin** or **Marietta**. Point out revenue history and outstanding AR. Explain: “Both are positive, so I cannot re-parent this old account. Approval creates a new current account under Bellhaven, then links the old account to it. The old parent and financial fields remain unchanged.”

**1:45–2:00 — Re-run safety**

Run the read-only scan again and show that identical decided proposals do not return to pending. Briefly show Decision history or the audit export.

## Before recording

- Do not display the personal API token or workspace access URL.
- Keep the local review app and the assessment's CRM browser ready in separate tabs.
- Start recording **before** the decisions you want to demonstrate.
- If a live approval fails, preserve the error/audit and fix the cause. Do not describe it as successful.
- The final video link must be accessible to the reviewers under the sharing setting you choose for submission.
